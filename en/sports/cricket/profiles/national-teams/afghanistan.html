The Afghanistan national cricket team represents Afghanistan in men's international cricket. Cricket took root among Afghans in refugee camps in Pakistan during the 1980s and 1990s, and the national team was formed in 2001 — the same year the ICC granted the Afghanistan Cricket Federation affiliate status.

Afghanistan's rise through world cricket is one of the fastest in the sport's history. Between 2008 and 2009 they won three consecutive ICC World Cricket League tournaments, climbing from Division Five to Division Three and earning ODI status in 2009. They qualified for their first 50-over World Cup in 2015, where they recorded a historic one-wicket win over Scotland — their first ever World Cup victory.

In 2017, Afghanistan were granted Full Member status by the ICC alongside Ireland, earning the right to play Test cricket. They played their inaugural Test against India in 2018 and claimed their first Test win in their second match, against Ireland in 2019. The team reached the semi-finals of the 2024 T20 World Cup and recorded memorable wins over England, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, and the Netherlands at the 2023 ODI World Cup.
