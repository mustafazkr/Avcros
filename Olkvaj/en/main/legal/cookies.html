Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026  •  Effective: September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Olkvaj ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website and services. It describes what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control their use.

By continuing to use Olkvaj, you agree to our use of cookies as described in this policy, subject to the choices you make in the cookie consent banner.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, remember your preferences, and provide information to the site's owners.

We also use similar technologies such as local storage and pixel tags, which function in much the same way. When we refer to "cookies" in this policy, we mean all of these technologies.

2. How we use cookies

Olkvaj uses cookies for the following purposes:

Essential Cookies Always On

These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function. They enable core features such as logging in, maintaining your session, protecting against security threats, and remembering your cookie consent choice.

You cannot disable these cookies, as the site would not work without them.

Analytics Cookies Optional

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with Olkvaj — which pages are visited most, how long users stay, and where they come from. We use this information to improve our content and user experience.

You can opt out at any time through our cookie preferences.

Preference Cookies Optional

These cookies remember choices you make — such as your preferred theme (light/dark), interface language, time zone, and data format. They personalize your experience so you don't have to reconfigure these settings on every visit.

Advertising Cookies Optional

Olkvaj displays advertisements through third-party ad networks. These cookies are used to show you relevant ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and measure the effectiveness of ad campaigns.

If you disable advertising cookies, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to your interests.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies on Olkvaj are placed by third-party services we use. These include:

  • Google Analytics — for understanding site traffic and user behavior.
  • Google AdSense — for displaying advertisements.
  • Payment processors — if you subscribe to a paid plan.

These third parties may collect information about your activity across different websites. We do not control these cookies — please refer to each provider's own privacy policy for more details.

5. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • Refuse non-essential cookies when you first visit the site.
  • Change your cookie preferences at any time via Settings → Data & Privacy.
  • Delete cookies already stored on your device through your browser settings.
  • Request access to, or deletion of, personal data we hold about you.

6. Managing cookies in your browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can usually find these options in the "Privacy" or "Security" section of your browser. Disabling cookies may affect how Olkvaj functions, and some features may not work correctly.

For more information about cookies and how to manage them, visit allaboutcookies.org.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our business practices. When we make significant changes, we will notify you by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where required, by showing a new cookie consent banner.
