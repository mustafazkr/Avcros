Ronaldinho is a former Brazilian professional footballer widely regarded as one of the most naturally gifted and entertaining players in the history of the sport. Known for his dribbling, creativity, flair, and free-kick ability, he won the Ballon d'Or in 2005 and was a central figure in Brazil's 2002 World Cup triumph.

Ronaldinho rose to prominence at Grêmio before moving to Europe with Paris Saint-Germain in 2001. His performances there earned him a transfer to Barcelona in 2003, where he became a global icon, winning two La Liga titles and the 2006 UEFA Champions League. He later played for AC Milan, Flamengo, Atlético Mineiro, and Querétaro before retiring in 2015.

Ronaldinho grew up in Porto Alegre and honed his skills in futsal and street football before joining Grêmio's youth academy. His brother and agent, Roberto Assis, was also a professional footballer and played a key role in shaping his early career. Ronaldinho's performances at youth level and in the 1997 U-17 World Cup marked him as one of Brazil's most exciting young talents.

Ronaldinho was born Ronaldo de Assis Moreira on 21 March 1980 in Porto Alegre, Brazil. He grew up in a working-class neighbourhood and lost his father at a young age. His older brother Roberto Assis, a professional footballer, became his mentor and later his agent, guiding his rise through the Brazilian youth system and eventually to Europe.

Off the pitch, Ronaldinho is known for his infectious smile and joyful style of play, which made him one of the most beloved footballers of his era. Since retiring in 2015, he has worked as a club ambassador, appeared in promotional campaigns, and remained a global icon of the sport. He was named FIFA World Player of the Year twice and won the Ballon d'Or in 2005.
